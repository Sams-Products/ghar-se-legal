Delete your account & data

Last updated: August 7, 2026 · Developer: Ghar Se

How to delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open the Ghar Se app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Deletion starts immediately after you confirm.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, email samsshah77@gmail.com from a reachable contact method tied to your account (or include your registered phone number). Subject line: Ghar Se account deletion request. We will verify ownership and process the request.

What is deleted or anonymized

  • Profile details (name, phone, avatar, city, and similar personal fields)
  • Seller profile content you control (bio, skills, visibility settings)
  • Your services and work posts (removed or anonymized so they are no longer linked to you)
  • Push notification tokens for your devices
  • Other personal content associated with your account, where feasible

What may be retained

Some order, payment proof, complaint, fraud-prevention, and safety records may be kept in anonymized or limited form where needed for legal compliance, dispute resolution, or platform safety. These records are not kept as an active personal profile you can log into.

Retention for those limited records is generally up to the period required for legal and safety purposes (and may be longer where law requires). Personal account access ends when deletion completes.

Partial data requests (without full account deletion)

You can update or hide some information in the app (Settings → Edit Profile), including options such as hiding phone number or exact map location for sellers. For other data questions, contact samsshah77@gmail.com.
