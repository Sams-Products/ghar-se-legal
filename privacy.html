Introduction

Ghar Se ("we", "our", or "us") operates a mobile marketplace that connects buyers with local home-based sellers across Pakistan. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use the Ghar Se app.

Information we collect

Account information: name, email address, phone number, city, and profile photo when you register or edit your profile. Seller information: neighborhood, bio, skills, services, work posts, and images you upload. Order & chat data: delivery addresses you provide for orders, payment method preference (for example cash on delivery), messages, and delivery proof images shared to complete an order. Usage data: likes, saves, profile views, service views, orders, messages, complaints/support messages, and in-app actions needed to run the platform. Device & location: approximate city from your profile or device location when you allow location access.

How we use your information

We use your data to create and manage your account, show relevant sellers and services in your area, process orders, enable chat between buyers and sellers, improve app performance, prevent abuse and fraud, handle complaints and support requests, and send important service-related notices.

Sharing of information

Your public seller profile, services, and work posts are visible to other users. Buyers and sellers who share an order or conversation can see the information needed to fulfill that relationship (for example order details and chat messages). We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted infrastructure providers that help us operate the app, including: • Supabase — authentication, database, and file-related backend services • Cloudinary — image hosting and delivery for photos you upload • Expo / app store platforms — distribution and basic app diagnostics We may also share information when required by law or to protect users and the platform.

Data retention & security

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or complete a deletion request. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices

You can update profile details in the app Settings. You can request account deletion in the app: open Settings → Delete Account. This submits a deletion request and suspends the account while we process it. You may also email support to ask questions about your data or deletion status. You may disable location permissions in your device settings at any time.

Children

Ghar Se is not intended for users under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
